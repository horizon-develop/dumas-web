## Design Context

### Users
B2B buyers: pet shop owners, veterinary clinic owners, and forrajería owners (large-scale animal breeding operations). They visit the landing page to evaluate Dumas as a wholesale supplier — they need to feel confident they're dealing with a serious, established distributor. They are not end consumers; they are business owners making purchasing decisions.

### Brand Personality
Reliable, local, serious. Dumas is a regional distributor with roots in the community — not a startup trying to look flashy. The brand should feel trustworthy and established, like a supplier that has been around and knows its trade.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm & lifestyle photography-forward, with the existing maroon red (`#8B0000`) preserved as the primary brand accent — it is part of the logo and non-negotiable. Avoid pure red (`#FF0000`) which is harsh and inconsistent. The overall tone should feel approachable and warm while remaining professional. Light theme. Not a tech startup aesthetic.

### Design Principles
1. **Warmth through photography** — let the imagery do the emotional work; overlays should enhance, not smother.
2. **Maroon as the one accent** — `#8B0000` is the brand. `#FF0000` and decorative reds are eliminated.
3. **Differentiate sections** — hero and ProfileAttention are currently structurally identical; they need distinct visual treatments so the page has rhythm.
4. **Serious over decorative** — no gimmicks, no sparkle effects, no glassmorphism. Typography and spacing carry the weight.
5. **Local & grounded** — copy and visuals should feel like a real Corrientes-based business, not a generic SaaS landing page.
